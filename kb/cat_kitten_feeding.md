# Kitten Feeding

Kittens grow rapidly and have higher calorie and protein needs than
adult cats. They are typically fed **3–4 small meals per day** of a
food specifically labeled for kittens or "all life stages," and most
owners transition to two meals per day around 12 months of age.
Fresh water should always be available, ideally in a bowl placed
away from the food bowl — many cats prefer not to drink right next to
their meal. Free-feeding dry food is common but makes it harder to
notice early appetite changes that can signal illness.
