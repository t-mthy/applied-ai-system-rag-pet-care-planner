# Puppy Feeding

Young puppies are typically fed **3 measured meals per day** until
about 4–6 months of age, after which most owners transition to 2 meals
per day. Portion size depends on breed, expected adult weight, and the
specific puppy food's calorie density — the bag's feeding chart is a
starting point, not a hard rule. Fresh water should be available at
all times. Puppies grow fast and can become hypoglycemic if meals are
skipped, so consistent timing matters more than for adult dogs.
