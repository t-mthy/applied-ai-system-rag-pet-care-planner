# Adult Rabbit Enrichment

Rabbits are intelligent and easily bored — a rabbit without enrichment
will find its own, usually in the form of destructive chewing,
furniture digging, or repetitive cage behaviors. Provide **safe chew
items** (untreated apple or willow wood, plain cardboard, woven grass
mats) and rotate them so they don't lose novelty. **Foraging puzzles**
— hay stuffed into toilet-paper tubes, treats hidden in shredded
paper — give the rabbit something to work for, which is closer to
natural behavior than eating from a bowl. Tunnels and hiding spots
also reduce stress, particularly for prey-instinct-driven rabbits in
open rooms.
