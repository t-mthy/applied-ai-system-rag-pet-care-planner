# Senior Dog Exercise

Senior dogs still need daily activity but in **shorter, more frequent,
lower-impact sessions** — typically two 15–25 minute walks rather than
one long outing. Avoid hard running, repeated jumping, and slippery
surfaces; these are tough on aging joints. Sniff walks (where the dog
sets the pace and explores scents) are excellent low-impact
enrichment for older dogs. Watch for signs of arthritis or pain —
reluctance to climb stairs, lagging on walks, or stiffness after rest
— and adjust intensity downward when they appear.
