# Kitten Play

Kittens learn how to interact with the world — and with humans —
through play. Aim for **multiple short interactive play sessions per
day** with wand toys, balls, and toys that move unpredictably; this
mimics hunting and channels the energy that would otherwise become
furniture-climbing or ankle-stalking. Hands and feet should never be
play targets, since the bite-and-pounce that's cute on a kitten is
painful on a 10-pound adult. End each session by letting the kitten
"catch" the toy so the play has a satisfying conclusion.
