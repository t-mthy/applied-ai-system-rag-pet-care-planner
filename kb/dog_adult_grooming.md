# Adult Dog Grooming

Brushing frequency depends on coat type — short coats need a quick
brush about once a week, while double-coated and long-coated breeds
need 2–3 sessions per week or more. Bathing once every 1–3 months is
usually plenty for most adult dogs unless they get visibly dirty;
over-bathing can dry out the skin. Dental care is the most
under-attended item: brushing the dog's teeth daily (or at least
several times a week) and checking gums helps prevent the dental
disease that affects most dogs by middle age. Nails should be trimmed
when they touch the floor as the dog stands.
