# Adult Dog Feeding

Healthy adult dogs are typically fed **2 measured meals per day**.
Portion size should be tuned to the dog's body condition, not just the
bag's chart — a dog you can feel ribs on with light pressure but can't
easily see is generally at a healthy weight. Treats and chews count
toward daily calories and should make up no more than about 10% of
intake. Fresh water should always be available, and food bowls should
be washed regularly.
