# Puppy Exercise

Puppies need short, frequent activity rather than long walks. A common
rule of thumb is roughly **5 minutes of structured exercise per month
of age, up to twice a day** (so a 3-month-old puppy gets about 15
minutes, twice daily). Over-exercising a puppy whose growth plates are
still developing can cause joint problems later in life. Free play,
sniffing, and gentle exploration count as activity and should make up
most of a puppy's movement; structured leash walking is the smaller
share.
