# Adult Dog Exercise

Most healthy adult dogs do well with **30–60 minutes of physical
activity per day**, split into two walks, plus shorter play, training,
or sniff sessions throughout the day. Working and herding breeds need
significantly more; some toy breeds need less. Mental stimulation
matters as much as physical exercise — a tired brain is at least as
calming as a tired body. Mix leash walks with off-leash play, training
games, and food puzzles to cover both.
