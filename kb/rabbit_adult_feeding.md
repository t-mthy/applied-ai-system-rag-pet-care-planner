# Adult Rabbit Feeding

Hay is the foundation of a rabbit's diet — **unlimited grass hay**
(timothy, orchard, or meadow) should be available at all times for
both nutrition and dental wear. A daily portion of fresh leafy greens
(roughly one packed cup per 2 lbs of body weight, varied across
several types) provides vitamins and hydration. Pellets should be
offered in **small, measured portions** (commonly about 1/4 cup per
5 lbs of body weight per day for adults), not free-fed; over-feeding
pellets is a common cause of obesity and gut problems. Sugary fruits
and starchy treats should be rare.
