# Adult Cat Feeding

Most healthy adult cats are fed **2 measured meals per day**, with the
total daily portion based on body condition rather than the bag's
default chart. Indoor cats tend to gain weight easily, and the
difference between "lean" and "overweight" is often only 1–2 pounds —
small but clinically significant. Free-feeding dry food contributes to
obesity and removes appetite as a useful health signal; portioned
feeding makes early changes easier to notice. Fresh water should be
offered separately from food bowls, since many cats prefer to drink
in a different location.
