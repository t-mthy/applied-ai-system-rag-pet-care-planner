# Puppy Grooming

The biggest grooming priority for puppies is **teaching them to be
handled** — paws touched, ears looked into, mouth opened, body
brushed. Short, calm, daily handling sessions during the
socialization window pay dividends for the dog's whole life. Coat
brushing 2–3 times per week is enough for most puppies but should
happen often enough that the puppy associates it with positive
attention rather than restraint. Nail trims and tooth brushing should
be introduced slowly and rewarded.
