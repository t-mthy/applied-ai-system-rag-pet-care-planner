# Senior Cat Feeding

Older cats often do better with **smaller, more frequent meals** —
two main meals plus a small midday portion is a common pattern.
Senior cats may lose interest in food because of dental disease,
declining sense of smell, or kidney issues, so unexpected weight
loss or appetite changes warrant a vet visit rather than a wait-and-
see approach. A senior or kidney-supportive diet may be appropriate
depending on the cat's specific health profile. Wet food becomes
more important with age because it both increases hydration and is
easier on worn teeth.
