# Senior Dog Feeding

Senior dogs typically continue with **2 meals per day**, but portions
often need to come down — older dogs are usually less active and put
on weight easily, and excess weight worsens joint and heart problems.
Many seniors do better on softer or smaller-kibble foods because of
dental wear, and some benefit from a senior-formula diet with
adjusted protein and joint-support ingredients. Sudden changes in
appetite, unusual thirst, or weight loss are early warning signs that
warrant a vet visit.
