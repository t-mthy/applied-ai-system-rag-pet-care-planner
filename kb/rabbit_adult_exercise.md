# Adult Rabbit Exercise

Rabbits need substantial daily out-of-enclosure time to stay healthy —
a common minimum guideline is **at least 3–4 hours per day** of
supervised free-roam in a rabbit-proofed space, and many house rabbits
live free-roam full time. Without enough room to run, hop, and
stretch, rabbits develop muscle weakness, gut motility problems, and
behavioral issues. Two longer sessions in the morning and evening
(when rabbits are most active) work well for most owners. The space
must be free of accessible cords, toxic plants, and small chewable
objects.
