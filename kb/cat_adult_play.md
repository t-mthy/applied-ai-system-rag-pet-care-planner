# Adult Cat Play

Adult cats — especially indoor-only cats — benefit from **at least two
short interactive play sessions per day**, totaling 20–30 minutes.
Wand toys, feather teasers, and laser pointers (always ending on a
catchable physical toy) tap into the cat's hunting instincts and
relieve boredom that otherwise shows up as furniture-scratching,
weight gain, or middle-of-the-night zoomies. Puzzle feeders are
useful between play sessions because they slow eating and add
foraging-style mental work. Most cats prefer multiple short sessions
to one long one.
